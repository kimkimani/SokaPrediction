## Our Commitment to Responsible Wagering

At **SOKA PREDICTIONS**, we believe that long-term betting success is impossible without strict personal discipline and risk management. We mandate that all users be at least **18 years of age** to access our prediction tools and VIP services.

### Core Principles of Disciplined Wagering:
1. **Never Bet With Money You Cannot Afford to Lose**: Never risk funds set aside for rent, food, healthcare, or essential bills.
2. **Treat Wagering as Entertainment**: Football is inherently unpredictable. No model or tipster can guarantee a 100% win rate.
3. **The 2% Bankroll Rule**: Never stake more than 2% to 5% of your total designated wagering bankroll on any single match or accumulator.
4. **Never Chase Losses**: Doubling stakes in an attempt to recover prior losses leads to emotional decision-making and rapid depletion of capital.
5. **Set Strict Time & Financial Limits**: Define daily, weekly, or monthly deposit caps and stick to them unconditionally.

### Recognizing Problem Gambling Signs:
- Spending more time or money betting than intended.
- Borrowing money or selling assets to fund wagers.
- Feeling anxiety, depression, or distress due to match outcomes.
- Concealing gambling habits from friends or family members.

### Where to Get Help:
If you or someone you know is experiencing difficulties controlling their gambling habits, confidential support is available:
- **National Responsible Gaming Hotline (Kenya)**: Contact local helplines or community healthcare centers for confidential counseling.
- **Support Contact**: You may request voluntary temporary or permanent account exclusion by messaging our support desk at **support@sokapredictions.com**.

### What is the legal betting age on Soka Predictions?
All users must be 18 years of age or older to use Soka Predictions or subscribe to VIP packages.

### What should I do if I am losing more money than I can afford?
Immediately pause all wagering activity, implement strict daily deposit limits on your bookmaker accounts, or request self-exclusion through our support desk.

### Does Soka Predictions guarantee profits?
No. Sports betting involves financial risk. Soka Predictions provides data-driven statistical analysis and probability models, but no match outcome is ever guaranteed.
