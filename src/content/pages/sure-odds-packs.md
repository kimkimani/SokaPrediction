## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are Sure Odds Packs?
Sure Odds Packs are standalone, targeted betting slips grouped by odds multipliers (e.g. 3+ odds, 5+ odds, 7+ odds). They allow punters to unlock a specific ticket instantly without a recurring membership.

### How quickly are the slips unlocked after M-Pesa payment?
The moment your payment is confirmed by Safaricom, the slip with full match details, bookmaker booking codes, and betting analysis is revealed on-screen and texted to your phone.

### What is the difference between 3+ Odds and 7+ Odds packs?
The 3+ Odds pack focuses on ultra-safe, low-variance fixtures (average 88% win probability). The 7+ Odds pack targets higher returns with 4 to 5 matches suited for higher payout multipliers.

### How are picks selected for each odds tier?
Our quantitative engine selects matches based on risk profiles: 2+ and 3+ Odds packs prioritize low-volatility fixtures, while 5+ and 7+ packs balance aggressive payouts with mathematical edge.

### Can I reuse the slip on any Kenyan bookmaker?
Yes! All picks feature universally recognized matches and standard betting markets playable on SportPesa, Betika, MozzartBet, Betway, 1xBet, or any preferred sportsbook.
