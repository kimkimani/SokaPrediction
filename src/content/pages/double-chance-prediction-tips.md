## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What does 1X, X2, and 12 mean in Double Chance?
- **1X**: The Home team wins OR the match ends in a Draw.
- **X2**: The Away team wins OR the match ends in a Draw.
- **12**: Either team wins (no draw).
