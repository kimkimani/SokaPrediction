## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How soon are yesterday's match results updated?
Match scores and prediction evaluations (Won / Lost) are updated immediately following the final whistle of each fixture and fully consolidated by 06:00 EAT.

### How do you evaluate whether a prediction was Won or Lost?
Predictions are evaluated against official regulation (90 minutes + stoppage time) final scores verified by official competition data feeds.

### Are lost tips ever removed or edited?
Never. Full transparency is our foundational rule. Every evaluated fixture remains permanently visible in our historical records to demonstrate our sustained 85%+ win rate.

### How can I use yesterday's results to improve my betting?
Reviewing past performance helps identify which leagues and markets (such as Over 1.5 vs BTTS) currently demonstrate the highest statistical stability and predictive accuracy.
