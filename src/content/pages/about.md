## Our Computational Prediction Methodology

Unlike amateur tipping blogs that rely on hunch or gut feeling, **SOKA PREDICTIONS** integrates directly with the **Sokaking sports analytics engine**. Our models ingest extensive historical and live match telemetry across more than 40 international leagues.

### 1. Poisson Distribution
Evaluates home offensive potency vs away defensive vulnerability to calculate precise goal probabilities.

### 2. Value Discrepancy Mining
Detects mispriced bookmaker margins where our algorithmic win probability exceeds the implied odds.

### 3. Variance Hedging
Identifies high-variance matches in SportPesa and Betika jackpots and suggests strategic double-chances (1X, X2).

Our system models expected goals (xG), recent team momentum, squad injury news, and market odds discrepancies using quantitative statistical algorithms.

No. We are an independent analytical sports tipping and prediction intelligence service. We do not accept bets directly.
