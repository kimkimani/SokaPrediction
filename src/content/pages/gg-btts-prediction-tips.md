## Mastering the Both Teams to Score (GG) Football Market

The Both Teams to Score (BTTS or GG) market removes the stress of having to predict the final match winner. Whether the game ends 1-1, 3-2, or 2-1, as long as both teams score at least one goal, the bet wins.

### What Makes a SokaPredictions GG Pick Stand Out?
- **Mutual Attacking Threat**: Both teams average over 1.4 goals scored per match across their previous 10 domestic outings.
- **Defensive Leakiness**: Teams that struggle to keep clean sheets (clean sheet percentage under 25%).
- **Head-to-Head History**: We give preference to rivalries where both sides have found the net in at least 4 of their last 5 direct meetings.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What does GG and BTTS mean in football betting?
GG stands for "Goal Goal" and BTTS stands for "Both Teams to Score (Yes)". Both terms mean the same thing: both the home team and away team must score at least 1 goal during regular time.

### What happens if a match ends 0-0 or only one team scores?
If a match finishes 0-0, 1-0, 2-0, or with any clean sheet, a GG / BTTS (Yes) bet is graded as lost, while a NG (No Goal) bet wins.

### What are the average odds for BTTS / GG picks?
BTTS odds generally range between 1.65 and 2.10. When two offensive giants clash (e.g. Arsenal vs Chelsea or Bayern vs Dortmund), odds typically sit around 1.70 to 1.85.
